Our Approach

How We Work

We're an AI-native life sciences market research firm built around a different engagement model: senior experts lead the work directly, while AI agents give them the execution capacity of a much larger team.

01 /

Led by Senior Life Sciences Experts

Every engagement is led by a senior life sciences expert with deep experience solving business problems across the full pipeline.

They don't just oversee the work. They scope the study, shape the research, work directly with the data, pressure-test the findings, and stand behind every insight and recommendation.

Your team gets senior expert oversight and judgment throughout the engagement.

02 /

Made Efficient Through Agentic Workflows

AI agents execute and iterate across the research workflow — building context, developing design materials, coordinating fieldwork, analyzing data, testing hypotheses, and synthesizing findings.

Our experts direct those workflows, challenging what emerges, and applying the judgment needed to distinguish an interesting pattern from an insight that should drive a decision.

The result is senior-led research amplified by AI: greater depth, more iteration, and faster execution.

03 /

Fielded Through the Method the Question Demands

We've built a research toolkit designed to get deeper, higher quality insights from every respondent — from AI-moderated interviews that probe intelligently and adapt in real time, to conversational surveys that uncover richer responses at scale.

The advantage isn't simply having more methods. It's knowing which one will produce the strongest evidence for the question at hand and combining them with traditional tools when the answer demands it.

The result is flexibility to match the business question and the depth to uncover what others miss.

Our Story

Who We Are

We have spent years advising leading life sciences companies on some of their most important decisions.

And we kept seeing the same limitation: traditional market research forces teams to make tradeoffs before the work even begins.

Go deeper, and you usually sacrifice scale. Add more questions, and the study gets more expensive and you risk data quality. Explore something unexpected, and you often need another round of research. Move faster, and rigor can suffer.

The result is that you routinely learn less than you could and wait longer than you should.

AI changes the underlying economics of research. It makes it possible to explore more questions, iterate more often, combine depth with scale, and compress work that once took weeks into days.

So we built 3e8 from the ground up around that possibility: senior biopharma experts, new research methods, and agentic workflows working together to produce deeper evidence with far greater speed and flexibility.
